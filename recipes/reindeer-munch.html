Reindeer Munch

Description

Easy and fun to make this snack with the kids. Ideal replacement to the cookies for Santa!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups crispy rice cereal squares
  • 2 cups thin pretzel sticks
  • 1 cup candy-coated milk chocolate pieces
  • 12 ounces of white vanilla baking chips

Instructions

  1. Stir cereal squares, pretzel sticks, peanuts, and milk chocolate pieces together in a large bowl.
  2. Melt vanilla baking chips in a microwave-safe glass or ceramic bowl in 30 second intervals, stirring after each melting, for 1 to 3 minutes.
  3. Drizzle melted chips over cereal mixture. Pour mixture out onto a large piece of aluminum foil and cool for 30 minutes. Break into bite-size pieces.
